I am going to Friday prayers. It's starting to rain,
so I'm going to get soaked with an umbrella. I
prayed and went out. The rain has stopped. I walked
home.
Where is the umbrella? Oh, I left it at the mosque. I
came and saw that there was no umbrella. Someone took it. I felt
sad. So I bought a big kachu
tree. I went home with an umbrella on my head. The
elder brother said, "Where is the umbrella?" I showed my elder brother the kachu
tree and said, "This is an umbrella." The elder brother was
angry. They married me to a coconut tree.
Those who pray five times a day should like this.
