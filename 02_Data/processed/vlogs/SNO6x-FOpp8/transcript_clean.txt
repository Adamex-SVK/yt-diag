Today my son tore the pillow so I
hung him upside down. Today my son was
operating on the lion, so I started scolding him
and snatched the lion away. So my son
tore the pillow in anger and came and lay down on the cot.
When my son fell asleep, I
brought a rope and hung it from the ceiling and
tied it to his leg. Just then my son woke up, so
I immediately pulled the rope and hung him upside down.
