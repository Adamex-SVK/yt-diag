ready by wearing a beautiful lehenga. Here mother was also
ready with soaked gram. Meanwhile
Tai ji also arrived. First of all I
took everyone's blessings and then we set out to
sow gram in our respective names. On the way
we also met a mother and I
took her blessings too. Then we reached the place where gram was being sown,
here everyone
sowed gram in their name one by one and
after sowing the gram, they shook them and pressed them well into the soil.
By the way, how many of you

exchanged our grams with each other and
