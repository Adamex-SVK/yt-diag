Here's how to make cold brew using only a jar and some cheesecloth. First, add coarse coffee grounds and cold or room temp water into the jar. I like to use four tablespoons for every six ounces of water. Give it a stir, then cover the jar with a cheesecloth and let it steep for at least 12 hours. When that's done, strain the cold brew through the cheesecloth and into a pitcher. Add one part concentrate and one part water over ice and enjoy a nice cup of cold brew. Cheers!
