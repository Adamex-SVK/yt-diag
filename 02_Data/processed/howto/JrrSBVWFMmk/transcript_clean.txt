Create a custom brush by painting a
white-on-black image in your favorite
painting app. Now, in your favorite 3D
app, connect two materials with a mix
shader node. Give it a new texture and
name it mask.
Go to texture paint mode. In the texture
tab, choose the brush mask. Load the
black and white brush that you created
earlier. In the active tool workspace,
set mask mapping to random. Check the
random option and increase jitter a
little bit. Select the mask texture so
that you can paint on it. Now, you can
paint edge wear using your custom
texture mask.
