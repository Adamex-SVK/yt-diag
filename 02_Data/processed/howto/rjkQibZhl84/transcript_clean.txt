This is the exact combo to get those
ribbed glass nails. After you apply your
dark base color, apply an even layer of
cat eye and wave your magnet in a
circular motion to get that spotlight
effect. Cure for 1 minute, apply matte
top coat, and cure for another minute.
And you're going to need a clear
non-wipe 3D gel for this design and the
longest liner brush you have. From
there, make as many thin stripes as you
can. Cure for a full minute and this
nail is all complete.
