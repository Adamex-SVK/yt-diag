Hi there, today I'll be showing you how
to get into chaturanga. Start in high
plank with your shoulders over your
wrists, shift your weight forward, bend
your elbows keeping them hugged in. To
make it easier, you can lower down on
your knees and lower down on your belly.
What you want to avoid is letting your
elbows splay out like this a push-up or
letting your belly sink.
Keep practicing and you will get there.
