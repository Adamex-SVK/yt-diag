here's how to make glazed donut holes
with just four ingredients mix together
greek yogurt and self-raising flour
until it forms a dough then flour your
surface and flatten it into a disk cut
into cubes or just pull off one inch
pieces and roll them into bowls bake in
the oven until light brown don't over
bake otherwise they'll go chewy mix
together icing sugar and milk for the
glaze then coat them in the mix and drip
off any excess best serve fresh and warm
enjoy
