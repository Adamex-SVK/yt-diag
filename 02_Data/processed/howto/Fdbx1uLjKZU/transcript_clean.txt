If you've never heard of quilt as you
go, I'm going to blow your mind. You put
your backing fabric onto some batting. I
just spray basted it on and then you put
a strip right sides together. You're
just going to sew this with a 1/4 in
seam allowance through all of the
layers. I pinned it just to make sure
that it would all stay in place. Trim
off the excess. I'm just going to finger
press this and then add another strip
same way right sides together. Pin it to
secure it and then stitch it with a 1/4
in seam. You're going to do this down
the whole length of your table runner
and tada, quilt
