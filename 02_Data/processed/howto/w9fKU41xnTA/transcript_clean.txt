So, you want to pour the perfect tulip.
First off, we set the cremer by pouring
from a height. Then, we move our jug in
and push in our first bulb. And after
this, we just repeat that process,
making sure we push into the middle of
the previous bulb. And as that cup
fills, remember to straighten it. Push
in one last bulb, lift up, and pull
through. Keep frothing.
