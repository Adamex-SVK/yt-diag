We will make a popcorn stitch We are working on a double crochet row. Make 4 double crochets into one stitch. One Two Three Four. Pull the stitch up a bit and take the hook off and go into the first one of these you just made. One, two, three, four. Insert the hook, put the stitch back, pull, take a yarn-over and pull through tight. Here it is. You can also make it using 5 double crochets.
