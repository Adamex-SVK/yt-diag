super easy jellyfish by taking a clean
brush and using clean water to paint
half a circle with a wavy bottom and
filling it in with water. Then I'm going
to drop in colors of yellow, orange, and
red, letting them move. Then I'm going
to create the arms by dragging the color
that's already there down the center and
then painting a roughly edge along the
edges of all three arms. Then I'm going
to create the tentacles by creating long
thin lines along the edges. Add some
splatters and you're done.
