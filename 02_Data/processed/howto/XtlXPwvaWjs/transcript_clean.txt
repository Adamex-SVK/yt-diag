My beautiful summer look explained simply for
you: First, I put my
hair up in a nice hairstyle.
Next, I take my
concealer, which I also use
as a base for eyeshadow. I simply dab a
little on my eyelid, blend it in with my
fingers, and then
set it with powder. With my eyebrow pencil,
I first comb through my
eyebrows and fill them in
where I
need a little more definition. I apply a matte, light eyeshadow
directly under my eyebrow to
visually lift it. On my eyelid, I apply
a really
beautiful matte rose shade – perfect for
summer! I also add a little rose shimmer to my eyes, specifically to
the inner corners of my eyes.
By the way, you can also
use the eyebrow pencil instead of eyeliner and
simply add
a little more definition to your lash line at the outer corners.
