Have you wondered how people create this crazy TikTok color picking trick? This is how to do it. Go to the Magic Wand and then pick Noise to create some noise, pick ridges and drag the noise up to around 100%. Then go to Glitch. To create a glitch effect, drag it all the way up to 100% and go for Diverge. Make sure you decrease the Red Shift, Green Shift and Blue Shift and drag the zoom to 0%. Now all you have to do is go to the Magic Wand again, pick Liquify, set it to Expand, set the Pressure to Max, Distortion and Momentum to None and with the size you can play around. Then tap your pen on the screen and just hold it there until you have one color. And that's how you create a pretty useless color picking trick. Have fun! Thanks for watching!
